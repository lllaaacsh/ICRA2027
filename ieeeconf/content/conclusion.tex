\section{Conclusion}
We proposed Trail, a prediction-based self-supervised representation learning framework for robot manipulation policies. The key idea is to estimate intent-aware latent residuals that encourage the model to focus on task-relevant changes, and then use these latent residuals for effective policy learning. Simulation and real-robot experiments demonstrate that our model benefits the learning for both continuous control and tabletop arrangement policies, outperforming prior methods. In the future, we will extend our framework to more diverse manipulation tasks and real-world robotic settings.
