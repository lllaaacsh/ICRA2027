\section{Conclusion}
We proposed Trail, a prediction-based self-supervised representation learning framework for robot manipulation policies. The key idea is to estimate task-relevant latent residuals and then use these residuals for effective policy learning. Simulation and real-robot experiments demonstrate that our model benefits the learning for both continuous control and tabletop arrangement policies, outperforming prior methods. In the future, we will extend our framework to more diverse manipulation tasks and real-world robotic settings.
